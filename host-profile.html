About the Laboratory

Apex Precision Tech Labs is a specialized high-density electronics service center equipped with Class 100 laminar flow benches, Weller and JBC micro-soldering stations, and FLIR thermal analyzers. With over 9 years of precision micro-repair, our certified engineers handle motherboard diagnostics that other shops turn away.

Every device admitted to our queue is handled on anti-static conductive mats with calibrated wrist grounding, and stored in nitrogen dry cabinets during overnight testing.
