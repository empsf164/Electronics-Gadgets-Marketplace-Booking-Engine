Our Mission

Re-Engineering How the World Services Electronics.

VOLTARA was created with a clear purpose: eliminate predatory repair shop pricing, banish counterfeit components, and provide a high-precision marketplace backed by escrow deposit security.

Customer Protection Standard

The 25% Deposit Escrow Standard

Traditional electronics repair forces customers into an unfair dilemma: pay 100% upfront to an unvetted local technician, or risk surprise billing upon device pickup.

VOLTARA revolutionizes this with Escrow Locking: You pay a small 25% deposit today to guarantee your bench slot and parts allocation. The remaining 75% balance is only released to the host laboratory after you physically inspect and approve the repair.

Zero Risk Guarantee If a host cannot resolve the diagnosed fault, your 25% deposit is refunded immediately.
The 5-Stage Laboratory Verification Protocol
Cleanroom & Tooling Audit
Inspection of Class 100 laminar hoods, thermal cameras, and micro-soldering stations.
ESD Compliance & Static Safety
Verification of conductive bench mats, continuous wrist grounding, and ESD humidity controls.
OEM Component Chain of Custody
Strict supplier vetting ensuring zero grade-C or counterfeit recycled ICs.
Technician IPC & ACiT Certifications
Proof of Master Rework (IPC-7711) or manufacturer board repair diplomas.
Continuous Fix-Rate Telemetry
Hosts failing to maintain a 98.5%+ first-time fix rate are automatically de-listed.
